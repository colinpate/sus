# Slayer filtered derived-log validation

Seven contiguous derived CSV logs were generated by `tools/front/create_slayer_filtered_logs.py` and selected through the `slayer-filtered` registry set. No dropout samples were removed or masked during pipeline processing. Each log uses the shared `slayer-filtered-alignment-v1` accelerometer rotation and its parent log's fixed magnetic baseline/reference through ordinary step configuration overrides.

All seven logs completed successfully through the normal front pipeline entry point. In particular, `log-0152-filtered-c01`, which previously failed independent accelerometer alignment, completed using its one stationary pair for static-offset correction and the shared fixed rotation for pod-to-pod alignment.

## Aggregate results

| Stage | Evaluation | Pooled RMSE | Pooled chunk-centered RMSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Acceleration | Unmasked | 14.20 m/s² | 14.17 m/s² |
| Acceleration | Dropout-masked | 11.92 m/s² | 11.82 m/s² |
| Magnetic | Unmasked | 24.05 mm | 9.23 mm |
| Magnetic | Dropout-masked | 22.21 mm | 8.12 mm |
| First fusion | Unmasked | 24.00 mm | 6.48 mm |
| First fusion | Dropout-masked | 22.36 mm | 6.21 mm |
| Final fusion | Unmasked | 24.15 mm | 5.99 mm |
| Final fusion | Dropout-masked | 22.55 mm | 5.66 mm |

Dropout masking was used only to compute the corresponding evaluation columns. The pipeline was trained and solved on all samples.

Compared with the earlier seven-window experiment that reused complete full-log preprocessing, the normal derived-log pipeline changes final pooled centered RMSE from 5.70 to 5.99 mm unmasked and from 5.28 to 5.66 mm masked. Nuisance correction improves the new normal-log aggregate from 6.48 to 5.99 mm unmasked and from 6.21 to 5.66 mm masked.

Absolute RMSE remains dominated by the approximately +41 mm offset in `log-0147-filtered-c02`; the fixed parent reference does not fully resolve that chunk's local magnetic-model branch ambiguity.
